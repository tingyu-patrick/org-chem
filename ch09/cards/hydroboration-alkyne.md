## 試劑
- 1. BH3, THF (or bulky R2BH such as Sia2BH / 9-BBN); 2. H2O2, OH⁻

## 家族
- syn：concerted syn hydroboration 接著氧化成 **enol（烯醇）**，再於鹼性條件下快速互變異構化（tautomerize）為羰基化合物。

## 中間體
- vinylic borane → enol ($\text{R-CH=CH-OH}$) → tautomerizes to aldehyde (or ketone for internal alkyne)

## Regio
- anti-Markovnikov (OH adds to the less sterically hindered terminal sp carbon → terminal alkyne yields an aldehyde!)

## Stereo
- syn in the hydroboration step (stereochemistry lost upon tautomerization to aldehyde/ketone)

## Rearrangement
- no (other than keto–enol tautomerization)

## 對比
- vs hgso4-hydration：末端炔（$\text{RC}\equiv\text{CH}$）經 `hydroboration-alkyne` 得到**醛（aldehyde, $\text{RCH}_2\text{CHO}$）**；經 `hgso4-hydration` 則得到**甲基酮（methyl ketone, $\text{RCOCH}_3$）**。兩者對於對稱內部炔（如 5-decyne）則都會給出相同的酮（5-decanone）。

# Ch10 Organohalides: Reagent → Reaction Index

> Core themes: **Radical stability** (`allylic ≈ benzylic > 3° > 2° > 1° > vinylic`); **Alcohol → Halide choice** (`3° ROH + HX` via SN1 vs `1°/2° ROH + SOCl2 or PBr3` via SN2 inversion without rearrangement); **Organometallics & Coupling** (`RMgX` = strong base/nucleophile, destroyed by protic groups; `R2CuLi` = Gilman coupling with `R'–X`; `Suzuki–Miyaura` = `Pd`-catalyzed `R–B(OH)2 + R'–X` coupling).

| Reagent | Reaction | Family (radical / cation / substitution / organometallic / coupling) |
|---|---|---|
| `1. 2 Li, pentane; 2. CuI, ether` | [Formation of Organolithium and Gilman (diorganocopper) reagents (`gilman-formation`)](cards/gilman-formation.md) | organometallic (RLi → R2CuLi Gilman reagent) |
| `1. Mg, ether (or THF); 2. H2O / H3O⁺ (or D2O)` | [Formation and protonolysis of Grignard reagents (`grignard-formation`)](cards/grignard-formation.md) | organometallic (strongly basic & nucleophilic carbanion equivalent) |
| `Cl2 or Br2, hν (light or heat)` | [Radical halogenation of alkanes (`alkane-halogenation`)](cards/alkane-halogenation.md) | radical (free-radical chain substitution) |
| `HF, pyridine` | [Conversion of 1° and 2° alcohols into alkyl fluorides (HF–pyridine) (`alcohol-hf-pyridine`)](cards/alcohol-hf-pyridine.md) | substitution (conversion of alcohol to alkyl fluoride) |
| `HX (HCl, HBr, HI), ether` | [Conversion of alcohols into alkyl halides with HX (`alcohol-hx`)](cards/alcohol-hx.md) | cation (SN1 via carbocation for 3°/allylic/benzylic alcohols; SN2 for 1°) |
| `NBS, hν, CCl4` | [Allylic (and benzylic) bromination with NBS (`allylic-bromination`)](cards/allylic-bromination.md) | radical (resonance-stabilized allylic or benzylic radical) |
| `PBr3, ether` | [Conversion of 1° and 2° alcohols into alkyl bromides (PBr3) (`alcohol-pbr3`)](cards/alcohol-pbr3.md) | substitution (SN2 backside attack with inversion of configuration) |
| `R2CuLi, R'–X, ether` | [Gilman (Diorganocopper / Corey–House) coupling (`gilman-coupling`)](cards/gilman-coupling.md) | coupling (organometallic C–C cross-coupling) |
| `R–B(OH)2 + R'–X, Pd(PPh3)4 (or Pd(OAc)2), base (CaCO3), THF` | [Palladium-catalyzed Suzuki–Miyaura coupling (`suzuki-coupling`)](cards/suzuki-coupling.md) | coupling (Pd(0) catalytic cycle: oxidative addition → transmetallation → reductive elimination) |
| `SOCl2, pyridine` | [Conversion of 1° and 2° alcohols into alkyl chlorides (SOCl2) (`alcohol-socl2`)](cards/alcohol-socl2.md) | substitution (SN2 backside attack with inversion of configuration) |

## 試劑
- NBS (N-bromosuccinimide), hν (light), CCl4

## 家族
- radical：NBS 在光與微量 $\text{HBr}$ 存在下維持極低濃度的 $\text{Br}_2$，避免對雙鍵發生親電加成，專一性地取代雙鍵鄰位（allylic）或苯環鄰位（benzylic）的 $\text{C–H}$ 鍵（BDE 最小，約 $364\text{ kJ/mol}$）。

## 中間體
- resonance-stabilized allylic radical ($\text{R-CH=CH-}\dot{\text{C}}\text{H}_2 \leftrightarrow \text{R-}\dot{\text{C}}\text{H-CH=CH}_2$) or benzylic radical ($\text{Ph}\dot{\text{C}}\text{H}_2$)

## Regio
- allylic / benzylic position ONLY; **unsymmetrical allylic radicals give a MIXTURE of allylic bromides** via both resonance forms!

## Stereo
- racemization at chiral allylic/benzylic centers; double bond often favors more stable (E)-isomer

## Rearrangement
- allylic rearrangement (double bond shift) occurs whenever the allylic radical intermediate is unsymmetrical!

## 對比
- vs `Br2, CH2Cl2` (Ch08)：`Br2` 高濃度時對雙鍵進行親電加成得 1,2-dibromide；`NBS, hν, CCl4` 則走自由基機構取代鄰位 $\text{C–H}$ 得 allylic bromide，保留雙鍵。

## 例子

- cyclohexene + NBS, hν, CCl4 → 3-bromocyclohexene (single product because allylic radical is symmetrical)
- 1-butene + NBS, hν, CCl4 → mixture of 3-bromo-1-butene and 1-bromo-2-butene (via unsymmetrical allylic radical)
- toluene + NBS, hν, CCl4 → (bromomethyl)benzene (benzyl bromide)
